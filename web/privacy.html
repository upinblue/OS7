Privacy

How this website handles data, under the GDPR.

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds nothing from a third party, and loads no fonts from anyone else’s server. Every file it uses — stylesheet, script, images, logos — comes from os7.org itself. Nothing you do here is measured, and nothing is passed on for advertising.

Hosting and server logs

The site is hosted on Microsoft Azure (Azure Static Web Apps), and the installation images are served from Azure Blob Storage. Delivering a page or a file to your browser necessarily involves your device’s IP address, the time of the request, the address requested, the amount of data transferred, and the browser and operating system your device identifies itself as. This is processed by the hosting provider on our behalf, as a processor under Article 28 GDPR.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in operating a website that works and in defending it against abuse. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors, and we do not combine it with any other source.

Microsoft is a US company with EU infrastructure. Where a transfer to a third country occurs, it is covered by the EU standard contractual clauses and Microsoft’s participation in the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. {{Confirm the region the resources are created in — the resources for this site are meant to be in a European region.}}

Your theme preference

If you use the light/dark switch in the header, the choice is stored in your own browser under the key os7-theme, using local storage. It contains the single word light or dark, it is never transmitted anywhere, and it is not written at all unless you click the switch. Clearing your browser’s site data removes it. Because it is strictly what you asked for and holds no personal data, no consent banner is involved.

Downloads

Downloading an installation image is an ordinary HTTP request to Azure Blob Storage and is logged in the same way as any other request described above. No registration, no email address and no form stands in front of a download.

If you email us

Writing to an address on this site means we process what you send — your address, your name if you give it, and the content of the message — for the purpose of answering you. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR where the message concerns a contract or its preparation, and Article 6(1)(f) otherwise. We keep such correspondence for as long as answering it and any statutory retention obligations require, and no longer.

Links that leave this site

Links to GitHub, Microsoft, Canonical and other sites are ordinary links: nothing is transmitted to them until you follow one, and from that point their own privacy notices apply. Nothing is preloaded, prefetched or embedded from them.
